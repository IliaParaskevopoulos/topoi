The question it answers

Where in the journey do customers struggle, and what should we fix or say there?

When to use it

  • Conversion or activation is lower than expected.
  • Planning content and onboarding for each stage.
  • Aligning marketing, product and support around the customer.

When to skip it

  • As a wall poster that nobody acts on. Map only to decide something.

How to run it

  1. Choose one persona and one journey.
  2. Define the stages from the customer’s side, not your funnel.
  3. Collect what they do, think and feel at each stage from interviews and data.
  4. Plot the emotional line and mark the low points.
  5. Assign an owner and a fix to the deepest low point.

Common mistakes

  • Mapping the journey you wish customers had.
  • Too many personas on one map.
  • Mapping without data on where people actually drop off.
