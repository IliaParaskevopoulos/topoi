Content Marketing Matrix

Sort content by whether it is emotional or rational, and whether it builds awareness or drives purchase.

Smart Insights, Dave Chaffey, around 2012

  1. Left to right: from awareness to purchase. Bottom to top: from rational to emotional.

    Nordhaus plots all its content from last year.

  2. Emotional content that builds awareness: stories, video, games.

    A video series on small-flat makeovers.

  3. Emotional content that moves people towards buying: stories, community, endorsements.

    Customer home tours with the pieces they bought.

  4. Rational content that builds awareness: guides, articles, reports.

    Moving checklists and storage guides.

  5. Rational content that helps people buy: demos, prices, comparisons, calculators.

    Room-planning tool, delivery prices, reviews.

  6. Plot your content and fill the empty quadrants.

    Nordhaus has plenty to educate and convince, little to inspire; it starts home tours.

The question it answers

Do we have a balanced mix of content types for awareness and purchase?

When to use it

  • Planning a content calendar.
  • Content that is all one type.
  • Briefing an agency on content needs.

When to skip it

  • When you have not yet decided audience and goals.

How to run it

  1. List your content from the past year.
  2. Place each piece in a quadrant.
  3. Compare the mix with your goals.
  4. Plan new content for the gaps.

Common mistakes

  • Entertainment with no link to the brand.
  • Only convince content, which reaches only people ready to buy.
  • Forcing pieces into quadrants they do not fit.
