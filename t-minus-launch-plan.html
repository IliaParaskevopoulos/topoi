Go-to-market and launch

T-minus Launch Plan

Count back from launch day, with phases and milestones, so everything is ready on time.

Common launch-management practice; no single author

  1. Around three months out: positioning, messaging, pricing and the launch plan itself.

    Tessera Predict: positioning and pricing agreed.

  2. Two months out: create the assets — website, demo, sales deck, case study.

    Demo, website page, pilot case study.

  3. One month out: train sales, partners and support, and brief press and analysts.

    Sales training, partner briefing, support guides.

  4. Launch day and the month after: campaigns run, results are tracked, and feedback is fed back.

    Launch event, campaign, daily pipeline review.

  5. Everything goes live together.

    Event, press release, email to all customers.

  6. About a month after launch: compare results with goals and decide what to adjust.

    40 demo requests against a goal of 50; the campaign shifts to plant managers.

The question it answers

What must be done, by when, for the launch to land?

When to use it

  • Any Tier 1 or Tier 2 launch.
  • Launches that keep slipping or arrive half-ready.
  • Coordinating many teams on one date.

When to skip it

  • Small Tier 3 releases, where a checklist is enough.

How to run it

  1. Fix the launch date and tier.
  2. List deliverables per phase and assign owners.
  3. Hold a weekly check-in against the plan.
  4. Run a go/no-go meeting a week before launch.
  5. Review results about a month after launch.

Common mistakes

  • Starting enablement too late.
  • No review after launch.
  • Moving the date without moving the plan.
