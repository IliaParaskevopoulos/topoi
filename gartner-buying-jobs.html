B2B Buying Jobs

B2B buyers do not move in a straight line. They work through six jobs, often going back and forth.

Gartner, 2019

  1. The buying group agrees there is a problem worth solving.

    Partners agree antibiotic prescribing must fall.

  2. They look at what kinds of solutions exist.

    Lab tests, rapid tests, clinical scores.

  3. They agree what the solution must do.

    Ten-minute result, reimbursed, validated accuracy.

  4. They compare suppliers against the requirements.

    Three rapid-test suppliers shortlisted.

  5. They check claims through references, trials and reviews.

    A two-week trial and calls with two practices.

  6. The group aligns on a decision. This often takes longest.

    Partners and practice manager agree on cost and workflow.

  7. Buyers move between jobs, so make help for each easy to find.

    Lumora adds a “buying guide for partners” to support consensus.

The question it answers

Are we helping buyers with every job they must complete to buy?

When to use it

  • Planning B2B content and sales enablement.
  • Deals stall even when buyers like the product.
  • Building tools for buying groups.

When to skip it

  • Simple purchases by one person.

How to run it

  1. Map your current content to the six jobs.
  2. Find the jobs with little or no help, often consensus.
  3. Create tools buyers can share internally: guides, calculators, comparison sheets.
  4. Make it easy for sellers to send the right help at each job.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the jobs as a linear funnel.
  • Content only for supplier selection.
  • Ignoring the internal work buyers do without you.
