Go-to-market and launch

Go-to-Market Canvas

One page that answers the core go-to-market questions: who, what problem, what value, what price, which channels, how you will measure.

Common go-to-market planning practice; no single author

  1. Who you will sell to first, specific enough to build a list.

    Paediatric practices in German-speaking Switzerland.

  2. The problem they feel most, in their words.

    Parents push for antibiotics before results are back.

  3. Why your product is the best answer for this segment.

    A result in ten minutes, during the consultation.

  4. The model and level, and how it compares with alternatives.

    Device rental plus CHF 12 per test.

  5. How buyers will hear about you and buy.

    Field sales, the paediatric association congress, and two distributors.

  6. The few numbers that show it is working.

    60 practices in 12 months; 300 tests per practice per year.

The question it answers

Do we have a complete, consistent plan for taking this product to market?

When to use it

  • Before a launch or a new market entry.
  • Aligning product, marketing and sales on one plan.
  • Reviewing why a launch is underperforming.

When to skip it

  • As a substitute for the detailed launch plan. It is the summary on top.

How to run it

  1. Fill each box in one or two sentences.
  2. Check the boxes agree with each other.
  3. Review it with sales and product leadership.
  4. Revisit it after the first 90 days in market.

Worked example: Lumora Diagnostics

Lumora Diagnostics is a fictional company that makes a rapid blood-test reader for GP clinics.

Channels

Success metrics

Common mistakes

  • A target segment too broad to act on.
  • Channels the target buyers do not use.
  • Metrics that measure activity, not results.
