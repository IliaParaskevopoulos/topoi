Sales enablement

Content-to-Stage Map

Map every piece of content to the stage where buyers and customers need it, and find the gaps.

Common product marketing practice; no single author

  1. Lay out the stages from first interest to growing account.

    Tessera audits 80 pieces of content.

  2. Content that helps buyers recognise the problem.

    A guide to the real cost of unplanned downtime.

  3. Content that helps buyers compare approaches.

    A comparison of spreadsheets, ERP modules and dedicated tools.

  4. Content that helps buyers choose and justify the purchase.

    ROI calculator, case study, IT security pack.

  5. Content that gets new customers to first value quickly.

    Set-up checklist and technician app videos.

  6. Content that helps customers get more value and buy more.

    Guides to adding a second plant and the Predict module.

The question it answers

Do we have the right content for every stage, and do sellers know which to use when?

When to use it

  • Sellers cannot find the right content.
  • Planning next quarter’s content.
  • Content focuses only on awareness.

When to skip it

  • When the stages are unclear; agree them first.

How to run it

  1. List every content asset you have.
  2. Assign each to one main stage and buyer role.
  3. Find stages with little or outdated content.
  4. Share the map with sales in the tools they use.

Worked example: Tessera

Tessera is a fictional SaaS company whose software helps mid-size manufacturers plan and track equipment maintenance.

Common mistakes

  • Plenty of awareness content, little for decision.
  • Forgetting customers after the sale.
  • A map stored where sellers never look.
