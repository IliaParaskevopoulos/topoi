Pricing and packaging

Pricing Models

The main ways to structure what customers pay, from a single flat fee to hybrids.

Common pricing practice; no single author

  1. One price for everything. Simple, but leaves money on the table with heavy users.

    A monthly fee per practice, any number of tests.

  2. Price per person using it. Easy to understand; can slow adoption.

    A fee per clinician.

  3. Pay for what you use. Fair and grows with value; harder to predict.

    A fee per test cartridge.

  4. Several packages at different prices for different needs.

    Starter, Practice and Group packages.

  5. A base fee plus usage, or packages with usage limits. Often the best fit, but keep it simple.

    Lumora chooses device rental plus a fee per test.

The question it answers

Which pricing model fits how our customers buy and get value?

When to use it

  • Launching a product or entering a new segment.
  • The current model causes friction in sales or renewals.
  • Usage and value vary widely between customers.

When to skip it

  • As a quick fix for low prices; the problem may be value, not model.

How to run it

  1. Map how value grows for customers.
  2. List which models fit that pattern.
  3. Model revenue and customer cost for typical accounts.
  4. Test the favourite with customers and sales before switching.

Common mistakes

  • A hybrid so complex buyers cannot work out the bill.
  • Copying a competitor’s model without checking the fit.
  • Switching models without a plan for existing customers.
