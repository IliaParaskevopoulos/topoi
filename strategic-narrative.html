Messaging and storytelling

Strategic Narrative

A sales story that starts with a big change in the world, not with your product.

Andy Raskin, 2016

  1. Open with an undeniable change in the world that affects your buyers.

    Large customers now pay their suppliers in 60 to 90 days.

  2. Explain that some will adapt and win, and others will fall behind.

    Small firms that wait run short of cash; those that unlock their invoices keep growing.

  3. Describe the desirable future, before you mention your product.

    Get paid the day you send the invoice.

  4. Introduce product capabilities as the tools that overcome the obstacles to that future.

    Connect your accounting software, pick an invoice, see one fixed fee.

  5. Show that it works: customers, numbers, stories.

    2,000 small businesses; median payout in six hours.

The question it answers

What story makes buyers see that they must change, and that we can help?

When to use it

  • Rebuilding a sales deck that starts with “about us”.
  • Launching a new category or a big repositioning.
  • Aligning leadership on one company story.

When to skip it

  • Short product pages, where buyers already know why they are there.

How to run it

  1. Agree the change in the world with leadership and sales.
  2. Write each of the five parts in one or two sentences.
  3. Build the deck around those five parts.
  4. Test it in real sales calls and adjust.

Worked example: Kontor

Kontor is a fictional fintech that lets small businesses get paid early on their invoices.

Name a big change
Large customers now pay their suppliers in 60 to 90 days.
Show winners and losers
Small firms that wait run short of cash; those that unlock their invoices keep growing.
Tease the promised land
Get paid the day you send the invoice.
Features as magic gifts
Connect your accounting software, pick an invoice, see one fixed fee.
Present evidence
2,000 small businesses; median payout in six hours.

Common mistakes

  • A “change” that is really your product.
  • Skipping winners and losers, which removes the urgency.
  • Too many features in the gifts section.
