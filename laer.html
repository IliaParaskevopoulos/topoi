Sales enablement

LAER

Handle objections in four steps: listen, acknowledge, explore, respond.

Carew International

  1. Let the buyer finish. Do not interrupt or prepare your answer while they talk.

    The category buyer: “Your price is above own-label.”

  2. Show you understood, without agreeing or disagreeing.

    “So price compared with own-label is the main concern.”

  3. Ask questions to find the real concern behind the objection.

    “Is it the shelf price, or the margin per metre of shelf?”

  4. Answer the real concern, with proof.

    Show that margin per shelf metre is 9% higher in test stores.

The question it answers

How should sellers handle objections without arguing?

When to use it

  • Training sellers and support teams.
  • Building objection-handling guides.
  • Price and competitor objections.

When to skip it

  • When the objection is a real deal-breaker; respect it.

How to run it

  1. List the ten most common objections from sellers and win/loss.
  2. Write explore questions and proof-based responses for each.
  3. Practise in role-play.
  4. Update the guide as new objections appear.

Worked example: Orto Foods

Orto Foods is a fictional maker of chilled ready meals sold through Swiss supermarkets.

Common mistakes

  • Jumping straight to respond.
  • Acknowledging in a way that sounds like agreement.
  • Responses with no proof.
