Sales enablement

BANT

Four quick checks to qualify a lead: budget, authority, need and timing.

IBM, 1950s–60s

  1. Can they afford it, or is there money set aside?

    The practice spends on diagnostics already; a test fee fits the budget.

  2. Are you talking to someone who can decide, or who can get you to them?

    The practice owner decides; partners must agree.

  3. Is there a real problem your product solves?

    Parents push for antibiotics before results are back.

  4. When do they need a solution, and is there a reason to act now?

    New prescribing guidance starts next quarter.

  5. Pass the lead to sales when enough checks are met. A later variant, CHAMP, puts challenges first, because need matters more than budget.

    Three of four met; budget to be confirmed. Lumora books a demo.

The question it answers

Is this lead worth a seller’s time right now?

When to use it

  • Qualifying inbound leads quickly.
  • Agreeing with sales what a qualified lead is.
  • Simple or mid-size B2B sales.

When to skip it

  • Complex enterprise deals; use MEDDIC.
  • As a script read to the buyer.

How to run it

  1. Agree with sales what counts as met for each letter.
  2. Ask about need first; budget questions land better after.
  3. Record answers in the CRM.
  4. Review qualified-lead quality with sales monthly.

Common mistakes

  • Disqualifying good leads because budget is not yet set.
  • Asking the four questions as an interrogation.
  • Treating a pass as a forecast.
